Five minutes a night. The email arrives in the morning, and you play the game at dinner. If you can eat together, you can do this.

It is designed for kids ages 6 to 12. Younger siblings love to join in, and older kids can lead the games for the family.

That is exactly who it is built for. Every game lets a shy kid take part at their own level. One word counts as a win on day one.
