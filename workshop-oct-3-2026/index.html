The hour

Sixty minutes. Your kid runs a tiny ice cream business.

1
Roll it. Make your own rolled ice cream at the bar. Pick the flavors, pick the toppings.
2
Price it. Kid math: what the ingredients cost, what to charge, the profit in every roll.
3
Pitch it. Name your shop, grab the mic, 30 seconds. The crowd votes. One kid leaves CEO of the Day.
